### FFmpeg and libmspack (LGPL)

Both are the sources pinned by ReXGlue SDK v0.10.0 (FFmpeg from `wmarti/FFmpeg` at `0604b464c7cb`), unmodified.
`patches/rexglue-sdk/0004` adds FFmpeg's configuration header for the Switch,
`thirdparty/ffmpeg-overlay/config_switch_aarch64.h`: the fork's Linux/aarch64 header (GPL and non-free parts off)
with what Horizon lacks turned off. It also builds the libraries
for the Switch in `thirdparty/CMakeLists.txt`. Everything needed to rebuild the
NRO and relink it with a modified version of either library is in this repository: [docs/building.md](docs/building.md)
and [docs/RELINKING.md](docs/RELINKING.md).
